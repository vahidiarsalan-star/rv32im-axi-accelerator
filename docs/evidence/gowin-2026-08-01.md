## Interpretation

Synthesis generated a netlist, but placement failed at the reset pin. This does
not establish routability, timing closure, Fmax, power or a working FPGA image.
The subsequent local process configuration had `SSPI: true`; that setting alone
is not evidence of a successful rerun. A fresh build must enable SSPI as regular
IO for T10 and retain its new reports.

The old report's resource numbers are deliberately not presented as current
utilization because its netlist predates the present RTL. See
[FPGA bring-up](../FPGA_BRINGUP.md) for the reproducible next steps.
